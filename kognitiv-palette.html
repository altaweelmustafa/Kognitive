Accessibility

Domain accents — dark and light sets each pass: lightness band for the surface, chroma floor (no washed-out hues), simulated protanopia/deuteranopia/tritanopia separation (worst pair ΔE 9.0 dark / 8.2 light, above the safe target), full-color separation (ΔE ≥15), and ≥3:1 contrast against the app background.

Brand fill uses a darker purple step (#6D3FD1) so white button labels clear 4.5:1 body-text contrast; the lighter #8A5CF0 stays for icons, rings and chart strokes where 3:1 is the right bar.

Trend colors reuse the universal green/red up-down convention and are always paired with an arrow + word, so they still read correctly for colorblind users or on a muted phone screen.
